# Acknowledgements

The PRF ecosystem exists in large part because of the generosity of organisations that believe in the work of nonprofits and ministries. Many of the tools we depend on are either fully sponsored, offered at no cost or have waived fees that would otherwise be out of reach. We are deeply grateful for each of them.

---

## TechSoup

[Kenya](https://www.techsoupkenya.or.ke/) · [Global](https://www.techsoup.org/)

TechSoup verifies our status as a nonprofit organisation and issues us a validation token that unlocks sponsorship access to most of the products listed below. Without TechSoup, many of these partnerships would not be possible.

---

## Google

[Google Workspace](https://workspace.google.com/) · [Nonprofits offering](https://www.google.com/nonprofits/offerings/workspace/) · [Google Gemini](https://gemini.google.com)

Through Google's nonprofit programme, our entire team operates on `@parkroadfellowship.org` email addresses with access to the full Google Workspace suite — including document management and 100 TB of shared storage. This means years of documents, photos and media from our missions can be stored securely without us worrying about physical hard drives.

Google Gemini powers the AI-generated mission summaries and insights in our application, helping leadership quickly make sense of the data we collect in the field.

---

## Microsoft Azure

[azure.microsoft.com](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-gb/)

Our server is hosted on Azure. The PRF API and supporting services run on Azure infrastructure, without which none of our digital operations would be reachable.

**Fees waived:** ~$2,000/year (~$166/month).

---

## GitHub

[github.com/parkroad-fellowship](https://github.com/parkroad-fellowship)

All our code is hosted on GitHub under an organisation account tied to fellowship-owned emails. This ensures that the codebase is not dependent on any single person and can be handed over smoothly as teams change.

---

## Cloudflare

[cloudflare.com](https://www.cloudflare.com/)

Cloudflare protects and accelerates our web presence. Their services provide DNS management, DDoS protection and performance benefits that keep our platform fast and resilient.

---

## Apple

[developer.apple.com](https://developer.apple.com/)

Apple waived the annual $100 Apple Developer Programme fee, allowing us to publish and distribute the PRF apps to members on iOS at no cost.

---

## Google Play

[Google Play Console](https://developer.android.com/distribute/console)

Google Play hosts our Android applications, making them available to members across Kenya and beyond.

---

## Flutter & Dart

[flutter.dev](https://flutter.dev/)

Flutter is the open source framework behind both the PRF Missions and PRF Leadership mobile apps. Building on Flutter means a single codebase supports Android, iOS and Huawei devices — without requiring a large development team.

---

## Laravel

[laravel.com](https://laravel.com/)

Laravel is the open source PHP framework that powers PRF Core, our backend API. Its ecosystem of packages — including Filament, Livewire and Sanctum — underpins the admin panel, real-time features and mobile API.

---

## Paystack

[paystack.com](https://paystack.com/)

Paystack processes payments and donations through our platform, enabling us to receive contributions from partners anywhere in the world.

---

## Advanta SMS

[advantasms.com](https://advantasms.com/)

Advanta SMS enables us to notify teachers and collect post-mission feedback via SMS, automating a significant part of our administration and follow-up process.

---

## Tomorrow.io

[tomorrow.io](https://www.tomorrow.io/)

Tomorrow.io provides weather forecast data that feeds into our mission planning, helping teams prepare for conditions on the ground before heading out.

---

## Canva

[canva.com](https://www.canva.com/)

Canva's nonprofit sponsorship gives our teams access to premium design tools and assets, enabling us to produce quality materials for missions, events and communications.

---

## Buffer

[buffer.com](https://buffer.com/)

Buffer allows us to manage and schedule content across our social media platforms. A few hours of preparation goes a long way, with posts properly queued across the days and weeks ahead.
