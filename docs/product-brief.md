# PRF — Product Brief

**Parkroad Fellowship Mission Management System**
*A purpose-built platform for managing evangelistic ministry to schools across Kenya.*

---

## What it does

The PRF SuperApp is an end-to-end platform that helps Parkroad Fellowship plan, run, and follow up on ministry missions to secondary schools and institutions. It connects field teams, leadership, finance officers, and students — before, during, and after every mission.

---

## Who it is for

| Audience | What they can do |
|---|---|
| **Leadership & Admins** | Plan missions, manage teams, approve budgets, and review performance |
| **Field Team Members** | Subscribe to missions, track activities, and submit reports from their phones |
| **Finance Officers** | Manage budgets, raise requisitions, record payments, and send financial reports |
| **Students & Cohort Members** | Enrol in follow-up training, track progress, and stay connected |

---

## Key Capabilities

### Mission Management
Teams can plan a full mission from start to finish — picking a school, setting dates, assigning roles, tracking sessions, and completing the debrief. Missions move through a formal approval workflow (draft → approved → complete) with the ability to cancel or reject at any stage.

### School Directory
A live database of schools with contact information, school terms, and a full history of past missions — making it easy to plan return visits and track relationships with each institution.

### Team and Member Management
Fellowship members can be organised into groups, assigned roles, and subscribed to specific missions. The system tracks both active members and those who attended without a user account, so headcounts are always accurate.

### Student Follow-up (Cohorts)
After a mission, students are enrolled into cohorts for ongoing discipleship. Each cohort is linked to courses, lessons, and modules so progress can be tracked over time.

### Events and Announcements
The fellowship can create and manage internal events, assign speakers, take subscriptions, and push announcements to all members through the app.

### Prayer and Spiritual Life
Members can submit prayer requests, respond to daily prayer prompts, and have their responses recorded — keeping the spiritual dimension of the ministry visible and trackable.

### Finance and Accountability
A structured financial flow covers budget estimates, requisitions with multi-step approvals, allocation of funds, payment recording via Paystack, and automated financial reports. Expense categories keep every spend accountable.

### Communications
Notifications are delivered automatically — by push notification to iOS, Android, and Huawei devices, and by WhatsApp to mission group chats. Teams are kept informed about approvals, updates, and cancellations without manual follow-up.

### Admin Panel
A full browser-based admin interface gives authorised staff access to all data, configuration settings, and operational controls without needing developer assistance.

---

## Mobile Apps

The platform is the backend for Android, iOS, and Huawei apps used by field teams. The API is versioned so existing apps continue working while new capabilities are rolled out.

---

## Key Automated Workflows

- Mission approved → team notified by push and email
- Mission completed → executive summary auto-generated
- Subscription confirmed → WhatsApp group link sent
- Payment received → reconciled and logged automatically
- School contacted → feedback request sent after mission
